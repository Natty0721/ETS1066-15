#For my today's practice in dictionary Methods, I choose the following: items(), pop(), and update()

## 1. items()
- Returns a view of all key-value pairs.
- Think of it as opening a diary and seeing all entries (both title and note).
- Useful when looping through both keys and values.

## 2. pop(key)
- Removes a key-value pair and returns the value.
- Like taking someone’s name off a guest list—you get the name, and they’re removed!
- Avoids manually deleting and accessing separately.

## 3. update()
- Adds or updates key-value pairs.
- Like refreshing contacts on your phone: existing names get new numbers, and new ones are added.
- Great for merging or refreshing data.
